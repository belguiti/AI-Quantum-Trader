🧠 XGBoost v2 · 12 Features

Enhanced ML engine with 12 technical indicators (RSI, MACD, ADX, ATR, EMA200, Stoch RSI, Williams %R, OBV, CCI, Bollinger, Volume Ratio). Uses Optuna HPO (20 trials) + TimeSeriesSplit validation.

Existing models use Continuous Learning — retains prior knowledge.

🌿 LightGBM · GOSS + EFB

LightGBM uses Gradient-Based One-Side Sampling (GOSS) and Exclusive Feature Bundling (EFB) for faster training with often higher accuracy than XGBoost on financial tabular data. Same 12 features, Optuna HPO.

Supports warm-start — extends existing model without forgetting.

🎭 XGBoost + LightGBM Soft-Vote

Trains both XGBoost v2 and LightGBM then combines their class probabilities (50/50 soft vote). Reduces individual model variance and captures both depth-wise and leaf-wise growth patterns.

⚠️ Trains two models — takes roughly 2× longer than a single engine.
